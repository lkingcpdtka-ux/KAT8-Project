================================================================================
CENTRAL WORKING HYPOTHESIS
================================================================================

KAT8/MOF loss collapses the adipocyte PPAR-fatty acid-oxidative metabolism
program equally in both sexes (~99% concordance), but estrogen/ERa signaling
buffers the downstream metabolic consequences in females by maintaining
partial PPAR-axis and insulin-response competence. The sex x genotype
interaction is not a separate gene program -- it is a magnitude tuning of
the SAME lipid/insulin handling genes, confined to ~2-4% of the
transcriptome.

REASONING FROM THE DATA:
  - 99-99.5% concordance; Pearson r = 0.61-0.73 --> same direction, same program
  - Variance decomposition: Genotype 17-33%, Sex 7-11%, Interaction only 2%
  - iWAT interaction genes (630): dominated by fatty acid metabolism, PPAR
    signaling, triglyceride metabolism, lipid storage, cholesterol
  - gWAT interaction genes (374): insulin response, dietary excess, lipid
    metabolism, lipoprotein handling
  - The interaction is WHERE estrogen is known to act (PPAR/lipid/insulin axis)
  - This is consistent with your own prior work (Diabetes 2022;71:198-LB)
    showing female C57BL/6 protection against metabolic effects of KAT8 KO

KEY TENSION TO KEEP IN MIND:
  The largest iWAT interaction class ("Female-amplified / Male-buffered",
  370 genes) shows females changing MORE, not less, on the PPAR axis.
  "Protection" may mean females activate compensatory lipid programs that
  males cannot -- not simply that females are unaffected. The transcriptomic
  magnitude does not directly equal phenotypic harm. Phenotype data (GTT,
  body weight, adiposity) must anchor the direction of "protection."
