# Results

## KAT8 loss disrupts the iWAT transcriptome more broadly than gWAT

To investigate the transcriptional consequences of KAT8 loss in adipose tissue, we performed bulk RNA-seq on iWAT and gWAT from KAT8KD and control mice. Differential expression analysis (DESeq2; FDR < 0.05, |log2FC| > 1) revealed a markedly asymmetric response: iWAT yielded 3,022 DEGs (1,867 up, 1,155 down), nearly three-fold more than gWAT (1,159 DEGs; 737 up, 422 down) (Fig. Xa). This disparity suggests that iWAT is substantially more dependent on KAT8-mediated H4K16 acetylation to maintain its transcriptional state, consistent with the distinct epigenomic landscapes and developmental origins of subcutaneous and visceral adipose depots. In both tissues, up-regulated genes outnumbered down-regulated genes (~62-64%), indicating that KAT8 loss not only silences direct acetylation targets but also triggers secondary transcriptional programs.

## Volcano plots highlight ECM remodeling and metabolic gene dysregulation

Volcano plots revealed that the most statistically significant genes in iWAT were uniformly down-regulated and related to mitochondrial and peroxisomal function, including *Hint2* and *Pxmp2* (Fig. Xb), foreshadowing the metabolic pathway collapse identified in downstream analyses. Among pre-selected genes of interest, *Mmp12* -- a macrophage-derived elastase -- was the most dramatically induced gene in both depots (log2FC = 4.40 in iWAT, 3.36 in gWAT), while its endogenous inhibitor *Timp4* was coordinately suppressed (log2FC = -2.67 in iWAT, -2.11 in gWAT) (Fig. Xc,d). This reciprocal Mmp12/Timp4 dysregulation, conserved across both depots, points to a fundamental shift in the protease-antiprotease balance favoring ECM degradation upon KAT8 loss. Additional MMPs were induced in a depot-specific manner: *Mmp16* and *Mmp14* were up-regulated in iWAT, whereas *Mmp3* and *Mmp14* reached significance in gWAT, suggesting that while both tissues engage matrix remodeling programs, the specific proteases deployed may reflect differences in resident immune cell populations or ECM composition.

## KAT8 is required for the mature adipocyte transcriptional program in iWAT but not gWAT

To examine candidate genes spanning adipocyte identity, ECM biology, and tissue remodeling, we generated focused heatmaps of 29 pre-selected genes of interest across both depots (Fig. X). In iWAT, the heatmap revealed a striking bifurcation. The master transcriptional regulators of adipogenesis -- *Pparg*, *Ppara*, and *Srebf1* -- were uniformly and significantly suppressed, along with their canonical downstream targets including *Fabp4*, *Plin1*, *Cd36*, *Lep*, *Sorbs1*, and *Ppargc1a* (Fig. Xe). The coordinate loss of both the upstream regulators and the effector genes they control indicates that KAT8 is essential for maintaining the mature adipocyte transcriptional program in iWAT. The suppression of *Ppargc1a* is particularly notable, as it directly links the adipocyte identity defect to the mitochondrial dysfunction identified in pathway analyses, and the reduction of *Lep* implies compromised endocrine output.

Conversely, fibrillar and pericellular collagens were induced in iWAT, led by *Col6a5* and *Col6a6*, while basement membrane collagens (*Col4a1*, *Col4a2*) were modestly reduced (Fig. Xf). Collagen VI is a well-characterized marker of adipose fibrosis that constrains adipocyte expansion and promotes metabolic dysfunction. The concurrent induction of *Fn1* reinforces the emergence of a profibrotic ECM program. Together with the MMP/TIMP imbalance described above, these data suggest that KAT8 loss drives active restructuring of the iWAT matrix from an adipocyte-supportive basement membrane architecture toward a fibrotic, collagen-dense stroma.

In gWAT, the same gene set told a different story. The adipocyte transcriptional program was almost entirely preserved -- *Ppara*, *Plin1*, *Fabp4*, and most metabolic genes were unchanged -- and collagen genes were uniformly non-significant (Fig. Xg). Yet the Mmp12/Timp4 axis was disrupted to a similar degree as in iWAT, indicating that the inflammatory remodeling response is shared between depots and is decoupled from the metabolic collapse seen only in iWAT. The divergent response of *Fabp5* -- suppressed in iWAT but induced in gWAT -- may reflect its high expression in activated macrophages, suggesting that the gWAT signal originates from infiltrating immune cells rather than adipocytes.

## KEGG pathway analysis reveals coordinated metabolic collapse in iWAT and a purely immune response in gWAT

Over-representation analysis (ORA) of KEGG pathways among down-regulated iWAT genes identified 41 enriched pathways, painting a picture of near-complete metabolic shutdown (Fig. X). The PPAR signaling pathway was the most significantly enriched (P_adj = 9.42 x 10^-11), encompassing *Pparg*, *Ppara*, *Adipoq*, *Scd1*, *Pck1*, and *Ucp1* -- the last indicating that the thermogenic capacity of iWAT is also compromised. This was accompanied by enrichment of virtually every major metabolic axis: fatty acid biosynthesis and degradation, carbon metabolism, the TCA cycle, glycolysis/gluconeogenesis, and branched-chain amino acid catabolism. Signaling pathways that coordinate adipocyte metabolism -- AMPK, insulin, glucagon, adipocytokine, and lipolysis regulation -- were likewise enriched, as were auxiliary metabolic programs (peroxisome, glutathione metabolism, cytochrome P450 detoxification). Collectively, these results reveal that KAT8 loss does not merely attenuate one metabolic branch but instead dismantles the entire metabolic architecture of the iWAT adipocyte.

Among up-regulated iWAT genes, ECM-receptor interaction was the top enriched pathway (P_adj = 8.47 x 10^-8), followed by cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction and cell adhesion molecules, consistent with active fibrotic remodeling and immune cell recruitment (Fig. X).

The gWAT pathway landscape presented a striking contrast. All 9 enriched KEGG pathways among up-regulated genes were immune-related -- dominated by cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction, chemokine signaling, IL-17 signaling, neutrophil extracellular trap formation, and C-type lectin receptor signaling -- pointing to activation of both innate and adaptive immune compartments (Fig. X). No pathways were enriched among down-regulated gWAT genes, confirming that KAT8 loss in visceral fat provokes inflammation without disrupting metabolic function.

## Gene set enrichment analysis identifies oxidative phosphorylation as the most coordinated transcriptional change

fGSEA, which leverages the full ranked gene list rather than a binary significance cutoff, reinforced and extended the ORA findings (Fig. X). In iWAT, oxidative phosphorylation was the single most strongly enriched pathway in either direction (NES = -3.25), followed by thermogenesis (NES = -3.12), indicating that mitochondrial electron transport chain dysfunction is the most coordinated transcriptional response to KAT8 loss. The TCA cycle, branched-chain amino acid degradation, peroxisome, and carbon metabolism pathways were similarly enriched among negatively ranked genes (NES < -2.7). Notably, fGSEA also detected ribosome down-regulation (NES = -2.82), not identified by ORA, suggesting translational suppression accompanying the metabolic collapse. Among positively enriched gene sets, osteoclast differentiation (NES = 2.61) -- which shares NF-kB and MAPK signaling modules with macrophage inflammatory programs -- ECM-receptor interaction, and cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction were the most prominent. The enrichment of disease-associated gene modules (non-alcoholic fatty liver disease, diabetic cardiomyopathy) further underscores that KAT8-deficient iWAT acquires a transcriptomic signature resembling metabolically dysfunctional adipose tissue.

In gWAT, 114 of 119 significant pathways had positive NES values, dominated by phagosome (NES = 2.53), osteoclast differentiation (NES = 2.44), natural killer cell cytotoxicity (NES = 2.41), and cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction (NES = 2.28) (Fig. X). Only 5 pathways were negatively enriched, including ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling (NES = -1.48) -- an intriguing finding given KAT8's role as a chromatin modifier, potentially reflecting cascading disruption of other chromatin remodeling complexes following loss of H4K16 acetylation. The absence of oxidative phosphorylation, thermogenesis, or PPAR signaling from the negatively enriched set confirms that the metabolic collapse is specific to iWAT rather than a general consequence of KAT8 loss in adipose tissue.

## GO Biological Process enrichment resolves the mitochondrial defect to multiple levels of organelle biogenesis (Supplementary)

fGSEA of GO Biological Process terms provided the most mechanistically granular view of the mitochondrial impairment in iWAT (Supplementary Fig. X). The three most strongly down-regulated terms -- proton motive force-driven mitochondrial ATP synthesis (NES = -3.20), mitochondrial translation (NES = -3.17), and mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I assembly (NES = -3.09) -- each represent a distinct level of mitochondrial function, indicating that KAT8 loss causes a comprehensive breakdown spanning energy production, organellar protein synthesis, and electron transport chain biogenesis. Fatty acid beta-oxidation (NES = -2.93) and lipid catabolic processes (NES = -2.37) were also suppressed. This multi-level mitochondrial failure is consistent with a model in which H4K16 acetylation is required at the promoters of nuclear-encoded mitochondrial genes, and its loss triggers a cascading bioenergetic deficit. Positively enriched terms in iWAT centered on leukocyte degranulation (NES = 2.77), cytokine production, leukocyte migration, and antigen receptor signaling, indicating that the immune infiltrate is not merely present but functionally active.

In gWAT, the negatively enriched terms were limited to cilium assembly, intraciliary transport, and tRNA metabolism -- none related to adipocyte metabolism -- potentially reflecting disrupted primary cilia signaling in adipocyte progenitors. The positively enriched landscape was dominated by immune processes: antigen processing and presentation (NES = 2.69), myeloid cell activation (NES = 2.62), TNF production (NES = 2.48), and leukocyte migration (NES = 2.26). The engagement of both myeloid activation and lymphocyte proliferation is consistent with a chronic inflammatory state involving innate and adaptive immune arms, rather than an acute response (Supplementary Fig. X).
