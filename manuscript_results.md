# Results

## Adipose-specific KAT8 knockdown elicits a stronger transcriptomic response in iWAT than gWAT

Bulk RNA-seq was performed on inguinal white adipose tissue (iWAT) and gonadal white adipose tissue (gWAT) from KAT8 knockdown (KAT8KD) and control (CTL) mice. After quality filtering and outlier removal (JS_08, JS_28), differential expression analysis using DESeq2 identified 3,022 differentially expressed genes (DEGs) in iWAT (1,867 up-regulated and 1,155 down-regulated; FDR < 0.05, |log2FC| > 1), compared to 1,159 DEGs in gWAT (737 up-regulated and 422 down-regulated; FDR < 0.05, |log2FC| > 1). These results indicate that KAT8 knockdown produces a substantially broader transcriptomic effect in iWAT than in gWAT.

## Volcano plot analysis reveals depot-specific patterns of differential expression

Volcano plots for both depots displayed the expected symmetrical distribution of fold changes, with dashed threshold lines demarcating significance (FDR < 0.05) and biological effect size (|log2FC| > 1) cutoffs (Figure X). In iWAT, the most statistically significant genes included *Hint2* (log2FC = -1.26, padj = 8.19 x 10^-73), *Gm44386* (log2FC = -4.70, padj = 4.85 x 10^-70), and *Pxmp2* (log2FC = -2.57, padj = 1.29 x 10^-63), all down-regulated, consistent with loss of mitochondrial and peroxisomal function. Among the genes of interest highlighted on the EnhancedVolcano plots, *Mmp12* was the most prominently up-regulated gene (log2FC = 4.40 in iWAT; log2FC = 3.36 in gWAT), while *Timp4* was the most prominently down-regulated (log2FC = -2.67 in iWAT; log2FC = -2.11 in gWAT). In iWAT, *Mmp16* (log2FC = 1.91, padj = 1.02 x 10^-3) and *Mmp14* (log2FC = 0.67, padj = 1.89 x 10^-9) were also significantly up-regulated, whereas *Mmp3* did not reach significance (padj = 0.062). In gWAT, *Mmp3* was significant (log2FC = 1.26, padj = 0.038) along with *Mmp12* and *Mmp14* (log2FC = 0.33, padj = 7.85 x 10^-3), while *Mmp16* was not differentially expressed.

## Genes of interest heatmap reveals coordinated suppression of adipogenic and metabolic programs in iWAT

A focused heatmap of 29 pre-selected genes of interest -- encompassing collagens/ECM components, adipocyte markers and metabolic regulators, and matrix remodeling enzymes -- was generated using variance-stabilized expression values (Z-scored relative to control means) and visualized with ComplexHeatmap. In iWAT, the heatmap displayed a clear separation between CTL and KAT8KD groups, with two dominant expression patterns. The first cluster comprised adipocyte identity and lipid metabolism genes that were uniformly down-regulated in KAT8KD samples: *Pparg* (log2FC = -1.54, padj = 5.66 x 10^-20), *Ppara* (log2FC = -2.15, padj = 2.38 x 10^-16), *Plin1* (log2FC = -1.61, padj = 5.57 x 10^-15), *Srebf1* (log2FC = -1.22, padj = 3.37 x 10^-15), *Fabp4* (log2FC = -1.02, padj = 1.17 x 10^-10), *Sorbs1* (log2FC = -1.07, padj = 2.18 x 10^-8), *Ppargc1a* (log2FC = -1.71, padj = 1.03 x 10^-8), *Lep* (log2FC = -1.04, padj = 6.22 x 10^-5), *Fabp5* (log2FC = -1.10, padj = 3.47 x 10^-6), and *Cd36* (log2FC = -0.93, padj = 2.46 x 10^-6). *Cav1* (log2FC = -0.77, padj = 4.90 x 10^-7) and *Angptl4* (log2FC = -0.59, padj = 1.72 x 10^-3) were also reduced, though with fold changes below the |log2FC| > 1 threshold.

The second cluster consisted of collagen and ECM genes that were up-regulated in iWAT KAT8KD samples. *Col6a5* showed the largest fold change (log2FC = 2.41, padj = 1.13 x 10^-3), followed by *Col6a6* (log2FC = 1.45, padj = 2.56 x 10^-7), *Col4a5* (log2FC = 1.17, padj = 1.34 x 10^-6), *Col13a1* (log2FC = 1.08, padj = 3.99 x 10^-5), *Col14a1* (log2FC = 0.99, padj = 2.73 x 10^-5), *Col16a1* (log2FC = 0.91, padj = 7.54 x 10^-6), and *Col27a1* (log2FC = 0.78, padj = 3.21 x 10^-5). By contrast, basement membrane collagens *Col4a1* (log2FC = -0.48, padj = 1.92 x 10^-3), *Col4a2* (log2FC = -0.56, padj = 5.28 x 10^-5), *Col15a1* (log2FC = -0.45, padj = 6.15 x 10^-3), and *Col5a3* (log2FC = -0.51, padj = 6.37 x 10^-3) were modestly down-regulated. *Fn1* was up-regulated (log2FC = 0.83, padj = 8.76 x 10^-9).

In gWAT, the same gene order was applied to enable direct comparison. The adipocyte marker and metabolic gene cluster showed no significant changes (e.g., *Ppara* log2FC = 0.006, padj = 0.99; *Plin1* log2FC = -0.39, padj = 0.11; *Fabp4* log2FC = -0.03, padj = 0.90), with the exception of *Pparg* (log2FC = -0.39, padj = 0.039) and *Fabp5* (log2FC = 0.71, padj = 5.46 x 10^-3; notably up-regulated in gWAT, in contrast to its down-regulation in iWAT). Collagen genes were uniformly non-significant in gWAT, with only *Col27a1* reaching significance (log2FC = 0.64, padj = 2.18 x 10^-3). Among the matrix remodeling genes, *Mmp12* (log2FC = 3.36, padj = 3.14 x 10^-9) and *Timp4* (log2FC = -2.11, padj = 2.36 x 10^-12) showed large and significant changes in both depots, while *Mmp3* (log2FC = 1.26, padj = 0.038) and *Mmp14* (log2FC = 0.33, padj = 7.85 x 10^-3) were significant only in gWAT at more modest fold changes.

## ORA of KEGG pathways identifies metabolic suppression and immune activation in iWAT

Over-representation analysis (ORA) of KEGG pathways was performed separately for up-regulated and down-regulated DEGs in each depot.

Among the 1,155 down-regulated DEGs in iWAT, 41 KEGG pathways were significantly enriched (adjusted p < 0.05). The most enriched pathway was PPAR signaling pathway (23 genes, adjusted p = 9.42 x 10^-11), which included core adipogenic regulators *Pparg*, *Ppara*, *Plin1*, *Fabp4*, *Adipoq*, *Scd1*, *Pck1*, and *Ucp1*. Additional metabolic pathways enriched among down-regulated genes included carbon metabolism (24 genes, adjusted p = 3.46 x 10^-7), valine/leucine/isoleucine degradation (16 genes, adjusted p = 4.03 x 10^-7), fatty acid metabolism (14 genes, adjusted p = 5.10 x 10^-5), fatty acid biosynthesis (7 genes, adjusted p = 7.03 x 10^-5), propanoate metabolism (13 genes, adjusted p = 4.03 x 10^-7), the citrate cycle/TCA cycle (7 genes, adjusted p = 9.80 x 10^-3), glycolysis/gluconeogenesis (10 genes, adjusted p = 0.010), and pyruvate metabolism (13 genes, adjusted p = 8.37 x 10^-6). The AMPK signaling pathway (24 genes, adjusted p = 1.41 x 10^-6), insulin signaling pathway (21 genes, adjusted p = 1.76 x 10^-4), adipocytokine signaling pathway (11 genes, adjusted p = 9.80 x 10^-3), glucagon signaling pathway (18 genes, adjusted p = 9.44 x 10^-5), and regulation of lipolysis in adipocytes (13 genes, adjusted p = 9.44 x 10^-5) were also enriched, consistent with a broad loss of adipocyte metabolic function. Peroxisome (15 genes, adjusted p = 5.16 x 10^-4), glutathione metabolism (12 genes, adjusted p = 8.83 x 10^-4), and xenobiotic metabolism by cytochrome P450 (14 genes, adjusted p = 1.08 x 10^-6) pathways were enriched among the down-regulated genes as well.

Among the 1,867 up-regulated DEGs in iWAT, 21 KEGG pathways were significantly enriched. ECM-receptor interaction (26 genes, adjusted p = 8.47 x 10^-8) was the most significantly enriched pathway, followed by neuroactive ligand-receptor interaction (36 genes, adjusted p = 3.89 x 10^-6), protein digestion and absorption (21 genes, adjusted p = 2.50 x 10^-5), cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction (31 genes, adjusted p = 0.013), and cell adhesion molecule interaction (24 genes, adjusted p = 0.019). PI3K-Akt signaling pathway (44 genes, adjusted p = 0.020), calcium signaling pathway (31 genes, adjusted p = 0.033), and cAMP signaling pathway (28 genes, adjusted p = 0.033) were also enriched.

In gWAT, ORA of the 737 up-regulated DEGs identified 9 significant KEGG pathways, all immune-related. Cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction (29 genes, adjusted p = 1.94 x 10^-7) and viral protein interaction with cytokine and cytokine receptor (17 genes, adjusted p = 2.17 x 10^-7) were the most significant, followed by IL-17 signaling pathway (12 genes, adjusted p = 3.70 x 10^-3), neutrophil extracellular trap formation (15 genes, adjusted p = 0.011), chemokine signaling pathway (18 genes, adjusted p = 0.013), tuberculosis (16 genes, adjusted p = 0.024), and C-type lectin receptor signaling pathway (12 genes, adjusted p = 0.045). No KEGG pathways were significantly enriched among the 422 down-regulated gWAT DEGs.

## fGSEA of KEGG pathways confirms metabolic program suppression in iWAT and immune activation in gWAT

Functional gene set enrichment analysis (fGSEA) using the DESeq2 Wald statistic as a ranking metric identified 165 significant KEGG pathways in iWAT (padj < 0.05). The pathways with the most negative normalized enrichment scores (NES) -- indicating coordinate down-regulation -- were oxidative phosphorylation (NES = -3.25), thermogenesis (NES = -3.12), valine/leucine/isoleucine degradation (NES = -2.93), ribosome (NES = -2.82), peroxisome (NES = -2.82), propanoate metabolism (NES = -2.81), carbon metabolism (NES = -2.80), non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NES = -2.80), citrate cycle (NES = -2.78), and diabetic cardiomyopathy (NES = -2.77). Among the positively enriched pathways, osteoclast differentiation (NES = 2.61), cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction (NES = 2.39), ECM-receptor interaction (NES = 2.39), and regulation of actin cytoskeleton (NES = 2.18) were the most prominent.

In gWAT, 119 significant KEGG pathways were identified, with a markedly different enrichment profile. Only 5 pathways had negative NES values, including mannose type O-glycan biosynthesis (NES = -1.95), ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling (NES = -1.48), and lysine degradation (NES = -1.62). The vast majority (114 pathways) had positive NES values. The most positively enriched pathways included phagosome (NES = 2.53), tuberculosis (NES = 2.53), osteoclast differentiation (NES = 2.44), natural killer cell mediated cytotoxicity (NES = 2.41), Epstein-Barr virus infection (NES = 2.37), cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction (NES = 2.28), and apoptosis (NES = 2.23).

## fGSEA of GO Biological Process terms corroborates depot-specific functional impacts (Supplementary)

fGSEA using GO Biological Process (GO:BP) terms with semantic similarity-based simplification (cutoff = 0.5) identified 43 significant terms in iWAT and 33 in gWAT (padj < 0.05).

In iWAT, the 13 terms with negative NES encompassed mitochondrial bioenergetics and catabolic processes: proton motive force-driven mitochondrial ATP synthesis (NES = -3.20), mitochondrial translation (NES = -3.17), mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I assembly (NES = -3.09), fatty acid beta-oxidation (NES = -2.93), mitochondrial electron transport NADH to ubiquinone (NES = -2.78), protein transmembrane import into intracellular organelle (NES = -2.58), ATP metabolic process (NES = -2.43), mitochondrial transmembrane transport (NES = -2.42), and lipid catabolic process (NES = -2.37). The 30 positively enriched terms reflected immune and structural remodeling processes: leukocyte degranulation (NES = 2.77), antigen receptor-mediated signaling pathway (NES = 2.38), positive regulation of leukocyte migration (NES = 2.33), positive regulation of cytokine production (NES = 2.28), regulation of actin cytoskeleton organization (NES = 2.08), and leukocyte activation involved in immune response (NES = 2.07).

In gWAT, only 3 terms had negative NES -- intraciliary transport (NES = -2.42), tRNA metabolic process (NES = -2.12), and cilium assembly (NES = -1.96) -- none of which relate to adipocyte metabolism. The 30 positively enriched terms were dominated by immune activation: antigen processing and presentation of exogenous peptide antigen (NES = 2.69), leukocyte degranulation (NES = 2.66), myeloid cell activation involved in immune response (NES = 2.62), positive regulation of tumor necrosis factor production (NES = 2.48), production of molecular mediator involved in inflammatory response (NES = 2.37), leukocyte apoptotic process (NES = 2.31), regulation of lymphocyte proliferation (NES = 2.29), and positive regulation of leukocyte migration (NES = 2.26).
